The former propagation instructions relied on an absent theorem and an invalid
crossing. They are superseded by these obligations.

| Curvature | Holonomy bound for a specified line connection | Smooth seed surface; identification with entropy; statistical coverage |

| Anomalies | Equal positive spectra of D*D and DD* | Gerbe, anomaly class, infinite-dimensional regularization |
| Operators | Initial-value invertibility; periodic multiplier-one test | NS analytic and nonlinear Hopf conditions |

| Zero knowledge | Matrix-free estimation is possible | Cryptographic protocol and security proof |

bound log determinants (D(t)=exp(t)). Spectral flow requires a specified
self-adjoint path. The historical Section 3 `.bak` is superseded archival text.
